Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

This policy covers PERMIT DENIED at permitdenied.app and its asset catalog, operated by Columbia Cloudworks LLC, based in Monmouth, Illinois, United States. Contact us at nicholas.king@columbiacloudworks.com about this policy or your privacy rights. Where applicable, Columbia Cloudworks LLC is the controller of information processed for these purposes.

The short version

The game is free, with no accounts, advertising, purchases, multiplayer, or user uploads. Gameplay runs in your browser. Optional analytics stays off unless you allow it and confirm that you are 18 or older. Declining analytics does not restrict the game.

Information used to deliver the site

Vercel hosts the site. Cloudflare provides network services and delivers catalog images and data, including through Cloudflare R2 at assets.permitdenied.app. Requests necessarily expose network information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time to service providers. They may process operational and security logs to deliver, protect, and troubleshoot the service. This necessary delivery is separate from optional analytics.

Optional analytics

With your permission, we use Vercel Web Analytics to understand traffic and improve the game and catalog. Reports may include page visits, approximate location, browser and operating system, device type, timestamps, and referring site. We remove query strings and fragments from analytics page URLs and restrict reported pages to the game, catalog, and policy pages. We suppress analytics when an external referrer contains a detailed path, query, or fragment.

We also record five limited events: starting a game (mode and district), reaching 60 seconds of active play (mode and district), finishing a game (outcome and active duration), restarting a game, and opening a catalog asset (its authored catalog ID). We do not send search text, map seeds, contact details, personal identifiers, or individual demolition actions as analytics event properties. Development, preview, automated capture, and test-map activity is excluded.

Vercel describes its visitor measurement as cookie-free, using a request-derived hash whose session identifier is discarded after 24 hours. That is not a promise that all analytics reports or operational logs are deleted within 24 hours. We do not enable Vercel's optional identifying features or create player profiles. See Vercel's analytics privacy documentation.

Your choices and browser storage

Allow and Decline are separate, unselected choices. Allow requires confirming that you are 18 or older. Players under 18 must decline. Use Privacy Settings at any time to withdraw or change permission; withdrawal stops future optional reporting but does not undo lawful processing already completed.

We store your analytics choice and the version of the terms you accepted in this browser's local storage. We do not collect a birth date or identity document. These preferences remain until you clear site data or change them; material terms updates may require a fresh acknowledgment. Preferences do not automatically carry across browsers or devices. If storage is unavailable, choices work for the current page session and may be requested again later. Game progress and debug settings are held in memory and are not saved as an account.

If you contact us

Emailing us shares your email address and whatever you include with us and our email providers. We use that information to respond, handle requests, and maintain necessary business records. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Purposes and legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for optional analytics; our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving reliable delivery for necessary technical processing and responding to inquiries; performance of an applicable agreement where necessary; and legal obligations where required. You can withdraw analytics consent without losing access to gameplay.

Sharing and international processing

We use service providers for hosting, asset delivery, analytics, and communications. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose information when legally required, to protect rights and security, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards. External links, including Facebook, GitHub, and our company website, have their own policies; this game does not embed a Facebook tracking pixel.

We are based in the United States, and providers may process information there and in other countries. Applicable contractual and legal safeguards govern restricted international transfers; contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your request. Provider details are available in Vercel's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Retention and security

We retain information only as needed for the purposes described, including resolving requests, security, and legal obligations. Provider retention and available analytics reporting windows depend on the service and plan; a dashboard reporting window is not a guaranteed deletion deadline. Aggregated analytics may outlast the visitor identifier used to produce it. We do not maintain a separate export of individual analytics events. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live and the information involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, receive a portable copy, object to processing, or appeal a request decision. Contact the email above to exercise applicable rights or appeal, without discrimination. We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request; we may be unable to identify a particular visit in aggregated analytics. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

Children and changes

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older, not directed to children under 13. Minors need parent or guardian permission under the Terms of Use. Optional analytics is limited to people who confirm they are 18 or older. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We will update this page when practices change and update its effective date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice or request fresh consent where required before using information in a new way.
