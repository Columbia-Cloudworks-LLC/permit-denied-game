# Release 1.0.43 verification

This release addresses #172–175, #177–182, and #184. The seasonal foundation shipped in 1.0.42; this release completes the overview vegetation, bounded-track cache, weather replay, and ambient detail transitions, and replaces the debug binder and device-selected HUD presentation.

## HUD space contract

Touch capability selects the joystick and blade only. Full instruments use 22 px cash digits and 38 px clock glyphs. Compact instruments use 18 px cash digits and 25 px clock glyphs, remove the permit badge, and put actions on a second row. Text is used only when the measured compact cash and clock widths plus 36 px of spacing exceed the available top panel width. Width is remeasured on container or instrument resize, including cash digit and hour rollovers. There is no user-agent or touch fallback threshold.

The isolated browser fixture checks both touch settings, cash from 999 through Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER, elapsed hours, rotation, overflow, and the exact pixel on either side of each transition. It also records the rollover and drives both touch controls in all three modes. Evidence is generated by `node scripts/verify-hud-instruments.mjs` against Vite at port 5178. Screenshots and a recording are in `artifacts/hud-instruments/`; `measurements.json` records actual CSS widths and modes. For ordinary six-wheel cash and a minute clock, initial samples retain compact instruments at 280 CSS px and use text at 240 px. Boundaries change with digit counts by design.

## Binder

The PD-01 Equipment field manual identifies its build revision and actual active map. Fold/Unfold and Close binder replace window iconography. A cover spine, three reinforced holes and ring hardware retain the sheet stack. Persistent colored divider leaves project beyond the content margin and have a non-color active state.

Controls remain native accessible inputs with custom inspection marks, stamped action borders, a readable hatched/struck disabled state, 44 px minimum targets, and a focus outline. Destructive/reset actions use a separate border treatment. Asset lists use a dropdown instead of a scrolling list printed on paper.

Numbered sheets are packed from measured indivisible controls and explanatory paragraphs. Containers, listeners, values, and simulation state survive page turns. Headings travel with the following control; legends remain intact. Previous/Next and PageUp/PageDown navigate sheets; primary tabs reset to sheet 1 and retain arrow/Home/End behavior. Escape closes the binder. This game has no gamepad input implementation, so no gamepad claim is made. An unfamiliar-person recognition review requires a human and is not represented as an automated result.

`verify-open-issues.mjs` records CSS viewport and DPR, verifies no clipping, and maps every available control to exactly one reachable sheet across desktop, tablet, and phone dimensions. `verify-debug-binder.mjs` exercises asset selection, damage/reset, overlays, freeze/step, upgrades, keyboard input, and folded touch driving through real sheet navigation.

## Seasonal contract and bounds

| Season | Weather and light | Surface evidence | Ambient sound | Gameplay effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | Seeded rain or clear; cool green light | Mud-colored ground tracks | Quiet filtered rain when raining | None |
| Summer | Clear; warm light | Restrained dust-colored ground tracks | No weather bed | None |
| Autumn | Seeded leaves or clear; amber light | Displaced leaf-colored tracks | Quiet leaf/wind noise when leaves blow | None |
| Winter | Seeded flurries or clear; cool light | Compressed snow tracks | Quiet high-filtered wind in flurries | Only the already-defined solid, traversable ice rule |

Weather uses an independent seeded stream. At most 36 particles survive, each for 1.6 seconds and within 16 world units of the vehicle. Off and Reduced release particles; Reduced retains subdued lighting/audio. Town replacement resets weather even with an identical seed. Audio reuses a single source and updates its gain when detail changes. Sound mute remains authoritative.

Season tracks reuse the existing mark system, keep at most 48 marks, skip pavement and water, and increment the overlay revision when old marks are replaced. They never alter collision or rewards. Winter overview broadleaf masses use low thickets and upright branch forks; conifer masses retain green sides with snowy tops. Species is derived from biome and seeded position independently of season. Drawing cache versions include season and species.

`seasonCoverage.test.ts` covers every campaign target in all four seasons without crop income, warm/winter lake/pond/bent-river traversal, and overview species/unchanged collision footprints. Existing season tests cover fixed geography, field stages, zero fallow payout, grid and geometry traversal, campaign retry, forest identity, and effects toggles. `seasonWeather.test.ts` compares replayed streams and runs ten minutes of particle steps to check bounds and cleanup.
